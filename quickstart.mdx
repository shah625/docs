<Warning>
Trading on updown uses **real money**. You can lose everything you spend on a position. You must be 18 or older and eligible under the Terms. See [Legal and eligibility](/help/legal-and-eligibility).
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Click **Login** in the top bar. On a phone, open the ☰ menu and tap **Login**. Sign in with your **email** (you'll get a code) or your **Google** account. The popup opens in place, and its footer reads "By logging in I agree to the Terms & Privacy Policy".

    Your account is created automatically, with a generated username such as "BlueAngrySquare" and a matching icon. You can change both later in [Settings](/account-and-profile#edit-your-profile).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Allow trading">
    Right after you sign up, a "Welcome to updown" popup shows the **Enable trading** panel: "One approval. After that, trades go through with no signing." It may also show a box for marketing emails: "Email me about new markets, promotions and product updates."

    Click **Allow trading**. The panel reads "Enabling trading" for a few seconds, then "Trading enabled". Click **Continue**.

    <Tip>
    If you close the popup, your first buy asks again, in a popup titled "One step before your first trade". Allow trading there, then place your order again. See [Allow trading](/account-and-profile#allow-trading).
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Deposit">
    Click **Cash** in the top bar. On a phone, tap **Deposit** on **Home**. The **Deposit with** dialog has two tabs:

    - **Cash**: **Deposit with card** ("Debit or credit · $50,000 limit") or **Pay with Cash App** ("Deposit via Cash App · $10 min"). Both open our payment partner's checkout, which shows its fees before you pay.
    - **Crypto**: **Send stablecoins** ("USDG, USDC, or USDT") shows your own deposit address. The other rows (**Transfer any crypto**, **Connect wallet**, **Pay with exchange**, and **Connect exchange**) go through our payment partner.

    With **Send stablecoins**, pick a network, then scan the QR code or copy the address. The address is the same on every network.

    | Network | Send | Minimum |
    |---|---|---|
    | **Robinhood Chain** (default) | USDG | $0.50 |
    | **Arbitrum** | USDC | $2.00 |
    | **Base** | USDC | $2.00 |
    | **Polygon** | USDC (native only) | $2.00 |
    | **Ethereum** | USDC or USDT | $20.00 |

    USDG on Robinhood Chain is cash as soon as it arrives, usually within seconds, with no fee. Deposits on the other networks are moved into your Robinhood Chain balance automatically, minus a small amount. A toast reads "Detected $10.00 USDC — adding to your balance…" and then "Deposit added to your balance".

    <Warning>
    Send only the assets listed for the network you picked. On Arbitrum, Base, Polygon, or Ethereum, a deposit below the minimum waits on that network until a top-up lifts it past the minimum.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make your first trade">
    You're already on the **Bitcoin · 5 min** market. At the top of the chart, **To beat** is the price this window must beat, **Now** is the live price, and **Time left** counts down to the close.

    In the ticket (the right column on desktop), pick **▲ Up** or **▼ Down**. Each button shows its price, such as "Up 51¢". Type an amount, or tap **+$1**, **+$5**, **+$10**, or **+$100**. The readout shows "Chance" and "To win": what you collect if your side wins. Tap **Buy Up · $5**.

    On a phone, tap **▲ Buy Up** or **▼ Buy Down** in the bar docked above the tabs. The same ticket opens as a sheet.

    Your order fills at the live quote. The ticket turns into a **Trade executed** card, for example "Bought $5.00 Up @ 52¢" and "Pays $9.48 if Up wins".

    <Tip>
    Turn on **⚡ 1-tap** for **$1**, **$5**, **$10**, and **$25** boxes that buy as soon as you tap one.
    </Tip>

    <Note>
    If you hold trade credit, it's spent before your cash. The card then reads, for example, "$3.00 credit + $2.00 cash · you keep the profit". See [Trade credit](/portfolio/trade-credit).
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a take (optional)">
    The **Trade executed** card offers **Add your take** ("Shown on the feed with this trade"). Type up to 280 characters in "Why this trade?" or pick a **GIF**, then tap **Post**. Or tap **Skip**. A take can't be edited once it's posted.

    **Share trade** opens a share card for your trade.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Watch it settle">
    When the window closes, the page holds it on screen: "Settling…", then "Up Won" or "Down Won". Winnings land in your cash on their own, usually within a minute or two. The bell shows a notice such as "You won $4.20".

    Tap **Go to Live** to trade the next window.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
On stock and crypto interval markets, the ticket also has a **Multiplier** slider. Above 1×, your order pays more the further the price closes your way, up to a cap, and less on a small move. Read [Multiplier](/interval/multiplier) before you try it.
</Tip>
