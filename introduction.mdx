updown is where you trade short-term price moves. It has one product: **interval markets**. Each market asks one question: will the price finish this 5-, 15-, or 60-minute **window** **Up** or **Down**? You can trade stocks, crypto, and **Pre-IPO** companies (OpenAI and Anthropic). Every winning contract pays $1.00.

Two tools sit inside interval markets:

- **Multiplier**: a 1× to 10× slider in the ticket on stock and crypto interval markets. Above 1×, your order pays more the further the price closes your way, up to a cap. A small move pays less, possibly nothing.
- **Bundle**: several interval buys placed at once. Each pick is its own trade.

Everyone opens on the **Bitcoin · 5 min** market, and the phone's **Trade** tab goes there too. To find another market, use **Search markets…** (**Ctrl K** / **⌘K**).

<Warning>
updown is **real-money trading**. Your cash is USDG, and losses are real. What you pay for contracts, or the stake on a multiplier order, is the most you can lose.
</Warning>

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **What you trade** | Up or Down on a stock, crypto, or Pre-IPO price over 5, 15, or 60 minutes |
| **What a contract pays** | $1.00 if your side wins, $0 if it loses. Prices run from 1¢ to 99¢. |
| **How a window settles** | On a 60-second value at the close: an average for stocks and crypto, a median for Pre-IPO. Up wins at or above the price to beat, so an unchanged close settles Up. |
| **Your money** | USDG in your own wallet on Robinhood Chain |
| **Most you can lose** | What you paid, or the stake on a multiplier order |
| **Hours** | Crypto and Pre-IPO: 24/7. Stocks: Sunday 8:00 PM to Friday 4:00 PM ET, and eight of them stay open through the weekend. |

## From sign-in to settlement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Click **Login** and sign in with your **email** or your **Google** account. Your account, wallet, username, and icon are created automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow trading once">
    A "Welcome to updown" popup asks for a one-time **Allow trading** approval. After it, trades go through with no signing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit">
    Click **Cash** in the top bar to open **Deposit with**. Pay by card or Cash App on the **Cash** tab, or send USDG, USDC, or USDT on the **Crypto** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a side">
    On **Bitcoin · 5 min**, pick **Up** or **Down**, enter an amount, and tap **Buy Up · $X**. Your order fills at the live quote, and **Trade executed** confirms it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a take (optional)">
    Say why you made the trade. Your take appears on the feed with it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it settle">
    Seconds after the close, the window shows "Up Won" or "Down Won". Winnings land in your cash on their own.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Window** | One round of a market. It opens on a fixed clock grid (:00, :05, :10… for 5 min) and closes 5, 15, or 60 minutes later. |
| **To beat** | The price the window must beat: the previous window's final 60-second value, not an instant price. Up wins if the close is at or above it, both rounded to 4 decimal places (DOGE: 7). Down needs a close strictly below. See [How windows work](/interval/how-windows-work). |
| **Now** | The current price, colored against To beat. On stocks and crypto it's a rolling 60-second average. |
| **Time left** | The countdown to the close. It turns amber once less than half the window is left, and red in the last fifth. |
| **Contract** | The unit you trade, priced 1¢ to 99¢. It pays $1.00 if its side wins. Contracts can be fractional, in steps of 0.01. |
| **Multiplier** | The 1× to 10× slider on stock and crypto interval markets. What you put in is your **stake**, and it's the most you can lose. See [Multiplier](/interval/multiplier). |
| **Bundle** | Up to 20 interval buys placed in one go, one pick per market. See [Bundles](/interval/bundles). |
| **Trade credit** | A promo balance that's spent before your cash. You keep the profit it makes, but the credit itself can't be withdrawn. See [Trade credit](/portfolio/trade-credit). |
| **USDG** | The dollar token your cash is held in, on Robinhood Chain. |

More terms are in the [Glossary](/help/glossary).

<Note>
Orders that trade right away pay a small taker fee, built into what you spend and what "To win" shows. Limit orders that wait and are filled later pay no fee. Nothing is charged at settlement. A multiplier order has no separate fee line; the payouts on the ticket already reflect the price. See [Placing orders](/interval/placing-orders).
</Note>

Around the markets, you can follow traders and copy their trades from the feed, earn an [ELO rating](/platform/leaderboards-and-elo), and compete in the [Trading Tournament](/platform/tournament).
