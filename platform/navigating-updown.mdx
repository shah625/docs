## Where you land

Open updown and you land on the **Bitcoin · 5 min** Up or Down market. Everyone starts there, signed in or not, on a computer or a phone. Click the **updown** logo any time to come back to it.

There's no separate home page on desktop. You start on a live market, with the side panel beside it on wide screens. See [The market page](/interval/the-market-page) for what's on it, and the [Quickstart](/quickstart) for a first trade.

What you see depends on the width of your screen:

| Screen | What you get |
|--------|--------------|
| Under 768 px (phones) | A logo row, a floating tab bar, and the ☰ menu |
| 768 px and wider | The top bar and the status bar |
| 1280 px and wider | The side panel as well |

## The top bar (desktop)

The top bar is one row. It has no section links. From left to right:

| Item | Shown to | What it does |
|------|----------|--------------|
| **updown** logo | Everyone | Opens Bitcoin · 5 min |
| **Search markets…** | Everyone | Opens [search](#search). You can also press **Ctrl K** or **⌘K** |
| Rewards bar | Signed in, if your account earns creator rewards | A gift icon, your points, then "Claim", or the points still to go, such as "380 left". Click it for the **Creator Rewards** popup |
| Bell | Signed in | Your notifications. A red count shows how many are unread |
| **Cash** | Signed in | What you have available to trade. Click it to deposit |
| **Credit** | Signed in, while you hold trade credit | Your unused trade credit. Opens your Portfolio |
| **Portfolio** | Signed in | Your cash plus your open positions at their current value. Opens your Portfolio |
| Avatar menu | Signed in | Your account links. Signed out, a **Login** button sits here instead |

Hover a figure for its tooltip: "Cash = available to trade · click to deposit", "Portfolio = cash + open positions", and for **Credit**, "Trade credit = used before your cash on every trade · you keep the profit it makes; the credit itself can't be withdrawn".

The figures show cents under $1,000 ("$3.85"), whole dollars up to four digits ("$3,181"), and a short form above that ("$14.5K", "$1.2M").

### Deposit

There's no separate **Deposit** button. Click **Cash** to open **"Deposit with"**. On a phone, tap **Home** → **Deposit**. See [Deposit and withdraw](/portfolio/deposit-withdraw).

### The rewards bar

The bar fills toward 500 points. Every 500 points turns into $5 of trade credit. Its tooltip reads "Make your first trade to start earning points", "380 pts to go until your next $5", or "Your points are ready to turn into trade credit". You can also open the popup from the avatar menu → **Rewards**. See [Creator rewards](/platform/creator-rewards).

### Notifications

Click the bell to open **Notifications**. The dropdown lists your newest 50 notices, and opening it marks them read. The count stops at "99+". With nothing there yet, it reads "Nothing yet. A follow, a copied trade, a like or a settled trade lands here." See [Notifications](/platform/notifications).

### The avatar menu

Click your avatar or name. From top to bottom:

| Item | What it opens |
|------|---------------|
| **Profile** | Your Portfolio page, which is also your profile |
| **Rewards** | The **Creator Rewards** popup |
| **Settings** | Your profile, visibility, emails, **Log out**, and **Delete account**. See [Account and profile](/account-and-profile) |
| **API keys** | Keys for trading through your own program. See the [API docs](/api/introduction) |
| **Team** | Let other people use your account. See [Team access](/platform/team-access) |
| **Use \{name\}'s account** / **Back to my account** | Switch into an account you've been let into, and back |
| **Light mode** / **Dark mode** | The theme switch. Dark is the default, and your choice is saved in this browser |
| **Log out** | Signs you out |

While you're using someone else's account, the top of the menu reads "Using \{owner\}'s account" with your role, "View only" or "Can trade".

## On a phone

### The logo row

At the top of the page sit ☰ on the left, the **updown** logo, and on the right the bell (signed in) and a search icon. The row scrolls with the page.

### The tab bar

A floating tab bar sits near the bottom of the screen:

| Tab | What it opens |
|-----|---------------|
| **Home** | Your balance, **Deposit**, and the **Trade · Feed · Leaderboard** tabs |
| **Trade** | The Bitcoin · 5 min market. It stays lit on any market page |
| **Portfolio** | Your profile and positions. It shows your avatar once you're signed in |

### Home

From top to bottom:

1. **Your balance** (signed in only): your ELO tier, such as "Bronze 3", which opens the ELO board; your portfolio value, which opens your Portfolio; "$X cash to trade"; and a green **Deposit** button.
2. **Trade · Feed · Leaderboard** tabs, opening on **Trade**:
   - **Trade** lists markets under the chips **Watchlist · Trending · Stocks · Crypto · Pre-IPO**. Each row shows its last 4 settled 15-minute results as ▲ / ▼, and opens the 15-minute market.
   - **Feed** is the live trade feed. If a trade-credit offer is waiting for you, a blue banner above it describes the offer. Tap ✕ to hide it. See [Trade credit](/portfolio/trade-credit).
   - **Leaderboard** matches the side panel's [Leaderboard tab](#leaderboard).

### The ☰ menu

Tap ☰ to open the menu:

| Section | Items |
|---------|-------|
| Top | A **Search markets…** field, and **Login** when you're signed out |
| **TRADE** | **Trade** (Bitcoin · 5 min), **Leaderboard** (the full ELO board), **Feed** |
| **PROFILE** (signed in) | **Profile**, **Rewards**, **Settings**, **Team**, **Back to my account** (while using another account), **Light mode** / **Dark mode**, **Log out** |
| **MORE** | **Contact us**, **Docs**, **Terms of Service**, **Privacy Policy**, **Your privacy choices**, and Discord and X icons |

<Note>
**API keys** are only in the desktop avatar menu.
</Note>

## The side panel (wide screens)

On screens 1280 px and wider, a panel runs down the left of the page. Drag its right edge to make it 300 to 420 px wide. Double-click the edge, or focus it and press **Enter**, to reset the width.

It has four tabs: **Watchlist · Trade · Leaderboard · Feed**. On a market page it opens on **Feed**. On other pages it opens on the last tab you used, or **Trade** the first time.

### Trade

Pick a chip: **Trending · Stocks · Crypto · Pre-IPO**. **Trending** is the default, and your pick is remembered.

| Chip | What it lists |
|------|---------------|
| **Trending** | Stocks, pre-IPO companies, and crypto together, biggest move either way first |
| **Stocks** | Stocks with interval markets, most volume first. Stocks that aren't trading yet fold under **No liquidity yet** |
| **Crypto** | Coins with interval markets |
| **Pre-IPO** | OpenAI and Anthropic. The chip shows only while pre-IPO markets are listed |

Each row shows a ☆, the logo, the ticker and name, the price, and the move with ▲ / ▼. Stocks show the move since the last close; crypto and pre-IPO show the move over 24 hours. Click a row to open that asset's 15-minute market. To open a 5- or 60-minute market directly, use [search](#search).

### Watchlist

Click the ☆ beside any row to star it. Starred assets appear in the **Watchlist** tab, under **Stocks**, **Pre-IPO**, and **Crypto**, biggest move first. On a phone they appear under the **Watchlist** chip on **Home** → **Trade**. With nothing starred, the tab reads "Click the ☆ next to a stock or coin to add it here."

- **Signed in:** your stars are saved to your account and follow you to other devices.
- **Signed out:** stars stay in that browser, and are added to your account the next time you sign in there.
- **Using someone else's account:** stars stay on your device.

### Leaderboard

From top to bottom:

1. A **Tournament** section while a Trading Tournament runs, and for three days after. It shows a status chip ("Starts in…", "Live · … left", or "Final"), the top six, your own line, and **Go to tournament page →**. Click the header to collapse it. See [Tournament](/platform/tournament).
2. **PNL** / **ELO** chips, and a **24H · 7D · 30D · ALL** range. **ALL** is the default. On **ELO**, a range ranks by the change in rating over that range.
3. The top 20. If you rank outside the top 20, your own row is pinned above #1 and tagged "You".

On **ELO**, **Full board →** opens the full ELO board. See [Leaderboards and ELO](/platform/leaderboards-and-elo).

### Feed

Pick **Global**, **Following** (signed in), or **This market** (on a market page). A **Top traders · 7D P&L** carousel at the top of **Global** shows the six best traders by realized P&L over 7 days, each with a follow button. See [Profiles and social](/platform/profiles-and-social).

## Search

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open search">
    Click **Search markets…** in the top bar, or press **Ctrl K** or **⌘K**. On a phone, tap the search icon or the menu's **Search markets…** field. The box reads "Search stocks, pre-IPO and crypto…".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type a ticker or name">
    Results are listed under **MARKETS**, one row per asset, such as "Bitcoin (BTC)" with "Crypto · Up or Down" beneath it. An exact ticker match comes first. Up to 50 markets are listed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a market">
    Click a row, or press **Enter**, to open its 15-minute market. To open another interval, click its **5 min**, **15 min**, or **60 min** chip on the right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Search covers only the stock, pre-IPO, and crypto interval markets listed right now. With nothing typed, it lists them all: stocks, then pre-IPO, then crypto. Use **↑** / **↓** to move and **Esc** to close. When nothing fits, it reads "Nothing matches “…”".

To see every market laid out at once, see [Browsing markets](/interval/browsing-markets).

## The status bar

On screens 768 px and wider, a thin bar runs along the bottom. From left to right:

| Item | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **Online** / **Offline** | Whether the app can reach updown. It checks again every 60 seconds |
| Price tape | Live prices for every asset with markets: crypto, then pre-IPO companies, then stocks by volume. Click one to open its 15-minute market. Hover over it to pause it |
| **Contact us** | The contact page. See [Support](/help/support) |
| **Docs** | These docs |
| **Terms** / **Privacy** | The Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy |
| **Your privacy choices** | Reopens the cookie banner |
| Discord and X icons | The updown Discord server and X account |

Phones have no status bar. The same links are in ☰ → **MORE**.

## Banners and notices

Banners appear above the top bar, in this order. None of them stay pinned; they scroll away with the page.

| Banner | When it shows | What it says |
|--------|---------------|--------------|
| Maintenance | While trading is paused | For example, "Trading is paused for maintenance. Resuming in 1h 30m. Join our Discord for additional updates". See [Service status](/help/support#service-status) |
| Tournament deadline | The last 6 hours of a Trading Tournament | A purple strip, such as "Tournament ends in 2h 14m · you're #1, defend it". It opens the tournament page |
| Trade-credit offer | Signed out, while a promotion runs | A blue line with the offer and a **Sign up** button. Click ✕ to hide it |
| Team | While you're using someone else's account | "You're using \{owner\}'s account · View only. Back to my account" (or "Can trade") |

You may also see:

- **The cookie banner** along the bottom of the screen, until you choose **Decline** or **Accept**. See [Cookies and privacy choices](/help/legal-and-eligibility#cookies-and-privacy-choices).
- **"Creator Rewards are here"**, a one-time popup for signed-in accounts that can earn creator rewards: "Earn points for being social — posting takes, liking, following and getting followed. Every 500 pts turns into $5.00 of trade credit."
- **One-time prompts:** **Allow trading** (see [Account and profile](/account-and-profile)), the updated-Terms prompt (see [Legal and eligibility](/help/legal-and-eligibility#accepting-updated-terms)), and the marketing-emails question. None of them appear while you're using someone else's account.

<Tip>
The theme switch is in the avatar menu on desktop and in ☰ → **PROFILE** on a phone. It shows only when you're signed in.
</Tip>
