**ELO** is updown's trader rating: "One number for how well you beat the market." Every trader has one, and the boards rank traders by ELO or by P&L.

## How ELO works

The in-app summary has three rules:

1. "Everyone starts at 100": "Bronze 3 · your first trades move it the most."
2. "Beat the odds": "Winning a long shot earns more than winning a favorite."
3. "Stay active": "P&L, volume, and sharing your takes all affect your rating."

In more detail:

- Your rating **starts at 100** (Bronze 3) and never goes below 0.
- Each interval position you **sell, or that settles**, is scored against the price you paid:
  - Winning a contract you bought cheap earns much more than winning one you bought expensive.
  - Losing a contract you bought expensive costs more than losing a cheap one.
  - Bigger trades count for more.
- Your first trades move your rating the most. Later trades move it less.
- On top of that, your rating gets limited credit for your all-time P&L (which can count against you), your lifetime volume, and the trades you post with a take or a GIF.

A settled or sold trade can take a minute or two to show in your rating.

## Tiers

There are seven tiers. Bronze to Diamond each have three divisions, numbered from the lowest: Bronze 1 is below Bronze 3. Prophet has no divisions.

| Tier | Range | Division 1 / 2 / 3 starts at |
|------|-------|------------------------------|
| **Bronze** | under 150 | 0 / 50 / 100 |
| **Silver** | 150 – 374 | 150 / 225 / 300 |
| **Gold** | 375 – 674 | 375 / 475 / 575 |
| **Platinum** | 675 – 1,049 | 675 / 800 / 925 |
| **Emerald** | 1,050 – 1,499 | 1,050 / 1,200 / 1,350 |
| **Diamond** | 1,500 – 1,999 | 1,500 / 1,670 / 1,840 |
| **Prophet** | 2,000+ | — |

Each rung has its own badge shape and colour. Hover a badge to see its range, such as "Bronze 1: 0–49".

## Your rating

Open your **Profile** page (avatar menu → **Profile**). The ELO panel sits at the left of the header, or in its own card on narrow screens. It shows:

- your tier badge and rating, such as "100 ELO"
- your rung, such as "Bronze 3"
- a bar to the next rung, with how far you have to go, such as "27 to Silver 1"
- a **?** button that opens "Trader ELO", a summary of the rules and the tier table, with a link to "See the full ELO leaderboard →"

Every trader's rating, rung, and win–loss record are public. The same panel shows on each trader's profile at `/u/username`.

### Your breakdown

Hover or tap your rating on your own Profile page to see what makes it up. Only you see your breakdown.

| Row | What it shows |
|-----|---------------|
| Heading | Your rung and its description, such as "Bronze 3 · Where every account starts." |
| **Record** | Your wins and losses and how many trades have settled, such as "14–9 · 23 settled" |
| **Avg entry (size-weighted)** | The average price you paid, with bigger trades counting for more |
| **Beating the odds** | What your scored trades add |
| **All-time P&L** | Your all-time P&L, and what it adds or takes away |
| **Volume** | Your lifetime volume, and what it adds |
| **Takes posted** | How many trades you've posted a take on, and what they add |

A footnote under the rows restates the rules and how far you are from the next rung.

### ELO chips

A tier badge with the rating sits beside traders' names on the side panel's P&L board, on [Tournament](/platform/tournament) rows, and in the trader suggestions on an empty **Following** feed. Feed rows show the badge alone. Hover or tap a chip for the rung, the rating, and the trader's overall rank, such as "ELO · #12". A trader with no interval activity has no chip.

## The side panel boards

The **Leaderboard** tab holds two rolling boards.

- **Wide screens:** the side panel → **Leaderboard**.
- **Phones:** **Home** → **Leaderboard**.

From the top, the tab shows:

1. The [Tournament](/platform/tournament) section, from before a tournament starts until 3 days after it ends.
2. The board chips **PNL** and **ELO**, and a range toggle: **24H**, **7D**, **30D**, **ALL**. The range applies to both boards and starts on **ALL**. Your board and range are remembered in that browser.
3. The board you picked, top 20.

### PNL board

The **PNL** board ranks traders by P&L on interval markets over the range, ending now.

- It counts windows that **closed** inside the range, with early sales included.
- It also counts your **open positions** at their current value, updated about every 2 minutes. Hover a figure for its breakdown. It ends "incl. +$Z on open positions" when open positions are included.
- When you traded with [trade credit](/portfolio/trade-credit), the P&L is your cash result.
- Multiplier orders don't count.
- Ties break on volume.

Each row shows the rank, the trader's avatar, name, and ELO chip, and their P&L. The top three ranks are coloured gold, silver, and bronze. With no trades in the range, the board reads "No settled trades in the past 24 hours." (or 7 days, or 30 days), or "No settled trades yet." on **ALL**.

There's no full-page P&L board.

### ELO board

On **ALL**, the line above the board reads "All-time ELO", and traders are ranked by rating.

On **24H**, **7D**, or **30D** it becomes the change board, headed "ELO change · past 24 hours" (or 7 days, or 30 days):

- Traders are ranked by **rating gained** over the range, the biggest gain first.
- Traders whose rating didn't move are left out.
- Each row shows the change, such as "+48", over the trader's earlier rank, such as "was #12", or "new" if they had no rating at the start.

Ties break on more scored trades, then P&L. **Full board →** on the ELO line opens the full ELO leaderboard.

### Your row and refresh

Inside the top 20, your row is tinted and tagged **You**. If you're ranked but outside the top 20, your row is pinned above #1.

| Board | Refreshes |
|-------|-----------|
| PNL | every 15 seconds |
| ELO | every 60 seconds |

Figures can lag by up to about a minute and a half, because ratings are recomputed about every 90 seconds.

## The full ELO leaderboard

The **Leaderboard** page (`/leaderboard`) ranks every trader by ELO. Its subtitle reads "Ranked by trader ELO", with the number of traders. Open it from:

- **Full board →** on the side panel's ELO board
- "See the full ELO leaderboard →" in the **?** dialog
- **See all** on the "Follow top traders" card
- ☰ → **Leaderboard** on phones

There's no link to it in the desktop top bar.

The page shows a podium for the top three, then your own card, then the list headed "Top 50 · ranked by ELO".

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **#** | Rank |
| **Trader** | Avatar and name, linking to the profile |
| **Record** | Wins and losses, such as "14–9" (hidden on phones) |
| **P&L** | All-time P&L in whole dollars, such as "+$1,234" (hidden on phones) |
| **ELO** | The rating |

On wide screens, a column on the right holds three cards:

- **Your rank:** your rung, rating, rank, and a bar to the next rung.
- **How ELO works:** the three rules above.
- **The ranks:** every tier with its range and divisions, with yours marked **You**.

On narrow screens, a **How ELO works** link opens the same summary. The page refreshes every 60 seconds. Before anyone has a rating it reads "No one is rated yet." and "Settle a trade to get on the board."
