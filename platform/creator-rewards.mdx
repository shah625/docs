**Creator rewards** pay you **points** for being social on updown: posting takes, liking, following, and getting followed or copied. Points turn into [trade credit](/portfolio/trade-credit). **1 pt = 1¢**, so every **500 pts = $5.00 of trade credit**. Nothing is paid in cash.

<Note>
Your points are private. Only you see them. They never show on your public profile, on a board, or in the feed.
</Note>

## Who can earn

You earn once your signed-in account has **made at least one buy**. What you did before your first buy counts once you make it. Until then, your rewards show 0 pts and the note "Make your first trade to start earning — what you've done counts once you have."

- Some accounts don't earn. They see "This account doesn't earn rewards."
- Only the account owner can claim. A teammate using your account through [Team](/platform/team-access) can't.
- Multiplier orders don't count toward creator rewards.

## Ways to earn

There are eight ways to earn. The **Pays** and **Fine print** columns quote the "See how to earn" slides.

| Way | Pays | Fine print | What counts |
|-----|------|------------|-------------|
| **Post a take** | "10 pts a take" | "On trades of $1.00 or more · first 5 a day" | A take on an interval buy of $1.00 or more. Your first 5 each day count. |
| **Add a GIF** | "+5 pts more" | "On the same takes" | Extra on those counted takes when the take has a GIF. |
| **Trade with a take** | "50 pts per $100" | "Prices from 10¢ to 90¢" | 0.5% of what you spend on every trade you post a take on, when you bought at 10¢ to 90¢. |
| **Like a take** | "1 pt a like" | "On other people's takes · first 20 a day" | A like on someone else's take. Your first 20 each day count. |
| **Get likes** | "2 pts a like" | "From people who have traded" | Each like on your takes from someone who has traded. |
| **Gain followers** | "10 pts a follower" | "Followers who have traded" | Each new follower who has traded. |
| **Follow traders** | "5 pts a follow" | "Your first 10 follows of people who have traded" | Your first 10 follows of traders. That's 10 in total, not 10 a day. |
| **Get copied** | "5 pts a copy" | "Once their buy goes through" | Someone copies your trade with the feed's **Copy** button and their buy completes. |

Each day's limits reset at midnight ET. Points count activity from 18 October 2026.

For example, you buy $20 of Up at 40¢ and post your first take of the day on it, with a GIF. That's 10 pts for the take, 5 pts for the GIF, and 10 pts for the trade: 25 pts in all.

Takes, likes, follows, and the feed are covered in [Profiles & social](/platform/profiles-and-social).

## Where your points show

| Place | How to open it | What it shows |
|-------|----------------|---------------|
| **Points bar** | The top bar, left of the bell (desktop and tablet) | A gift icon, your points (such as "120 pts") over a progress bar toward 500, and **Claim** once you can claim, or how many points are left, such as "380 left". Hover it for "380 pts to go until your next $5". |
| **Rewards popup** | Click the points bar, or avatar menu → **Rewards**. On phones: ☰ → Profile → **Rewards**. | Your points and "earned so far", a progress bar, a line such as "380 pts to go · 500 pts = $5.00 of trade credit", a status line, **See how to earn**, and the Claim button. Its footer reads "Credit is used before your cash, and you keep the profit." |
| **Creator reward card** | Your **Profile** page (avatar menu → **Profile**). On phones it sits above the tabs. | Your points, "500 pts = $5.00 of trade credit", a **See how to earn** button, the Claim button, and a progress bar. |

When you can claim, the line reads "Ready: 500 pts → $5.00".

## See how to earn

**See how to earn** opens a slideshow with one slide per way to earn. Each slide shows its number, such as "1 of 8", the way, what it pays, how it works, and the fine print. Its footer shows what you've earned that way so far, such as "You've earned 30 pts · ×3".

Move between slides with the ‹ and › arrows, the dots, the ← and → keys, or a swipe. The last slide, "Every 500 pts = $5.00", sums up: each way shows the points it has earned you, or its rate if it hasn't earned anything yet. Tap **Got it** to close.

The first time you open updown signed in, the slideshow opens on its own, with a welcome slide headed "Creator Rewards are here". It shows once per account in each browser.

<Tip>
If you had unclaimed cash creator rewards from before 18 October 2026, the summary slide lists them as points under "Carried over from cash rewards".
</Tip>

## Claim your points

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reach 500 pts">
    Below 500 pts the button reads **Claim at 500 pts** and can't be pressed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim">
    Tap **Claim $5.00** in the Rewards popup or on the Creator reward card. With 1,000 pts or more it reads **Claim $10.00**, and so on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your trade credit">
    The status line reads "500 pts → $5.00 added to your trade credit". The bell shows "$5.00 trade credit added": "You turned 500 pts into trade credit. It's used before your cash, and you keep the profit."
  </Step>
</Steps>

- A claim converts **whole 500-pt blocks**, up to **10,000 pts ($100.00)** per claim. Any points left over stay on your balance.
- The credit appears as "Creator reward" in your trade-credit history.

If a claim doesn't go through, the status line says why:

| Message | What it means |
|---------|---------------|
| "Claims are paused right now — try again later." | Claims are paused during platform maintenance. |
| "An earlier reward is still on its way — try again in a few minutes." | Your last claim is still being added. |
| "That claim was already made — check your trade credit." | The claim went through the first time. |
| "Rewards are unavailable right now." | Your rewards couldn't be loaded. |
| "Couldn't claim right now." | Something else went wrong. Try again later. |

## Reward credit is trade credit

What you claim is [trade credit](/portfolio/trade-credit):

- It's used before your cash when you trade.
- You keep the profit it makes.
- It can't be withdrawn.

The Trade credit page lists which trades use credit and what happens to it when a trade wins, loses, or is sold. Creator rewards are a promotion under the "Trade credit, rewards and promotions" section of the [Terms of Service](https://www.updown.fast/terms#promotions).
