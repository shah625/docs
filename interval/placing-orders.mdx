You trade an interval market from its **ticket**. On a wide screen the ticket sits in the right rail of [the market page](/interval/the-market-page). Everywhere else, tapping a price opens the same ticket in a **trade dialog**. Each contract pays $1 if its side wins. See [How windows work](/interval/how-windows-work).

## Before your first order

- **Signed out:** the sign-in popup opens first. Once you sign in, the ticket appears in place.
- **First trade:** if you haven't accepted the Terms or allowed trading yet, those popups open instead of the ticket. The trading one is titled "One step before your first trade". Tap **Allow trading**, then place your order again. See [Account and profile](/account-and-profile).

## The ticket

| Element | Label | Meaning |
|---------|-------|---------|
| **Tabs** | "Buy" / "Sell" | Buy contracts, or sell contracts you hold |
| **ⓘ** | | How an unchanged window settles |
| **Switch** | "⚡ 1-tap" | Amount boxes that buy at once (Buy and Market only) |
| **Dropdown** | "Market ▾" | The order type: **Market** or **Limit** |
| **Side buttons** | "▲ Up 51¢" / "▼ Down 49¢" | The price to buy each side now. The picked side is filled in its colour. |
| **Amount** | "$" box, "Enter amount", then "Amount" or "Sell value", over "$123.45 cash" | Dollars to spend or to sell, and the cash you can spend |
| **Chips** | "+$1" "+$5" "+$10" "+$100", "Clear" | Add to the amount, or reset it |
| **Readout** | "Chance 51%", "To win $9.48" with "+$4.48" | The side's chance, and what you get back if it wins. Sell shows "You sell 9.80 contracts". |
| **Button** | "Buy Up · $5" / "Sell Up · $5" / "Buy Up · limit" | Places the order |
| **Footer** | "By trading, you agree to the Terms of Use." | Links to the Terms |

**Up** is picked when the ticket opens, unless you opened it from a **Down** button.

## Buy at the live quote

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a side">
    Keep **Buy** and **Market** selected. Tap **▲ Up** or **▼ Down**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount">
    Type dollars in the **$** box, or tap **+$1**, **+$5**, **+$10**, or **+$100** to build it up. There is no minimum.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the readout">
    **Chance** is the midpoint between the price to buy and the price to sell that side. **To win** is what you get back if your side wins, and the green figure beside it is your profit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy">
    Tap **Buy Up · $5**. The button reads "Placing order…", or "Still confirming…" after 5 seconds. When it fills, the [**Trade executed**](#confirmation) card appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### 1-tap

Turn on **⚡ 1-tap** to buy with one tap. It's off each time you open the ticket.

- Four boxes, **$1**, **$5**, **$10**, and **$25**, each show what they win ("win $9.48"). Tapping one buys at once.
- Or type an amount in **Custom** and tap **Buy**.
- The line under the boxes reads "Tap an amount to buy Up". While the order is out it reads "Placing order…", and after 5 seconds "Still confirming — this is taking longer than usual. Your position will update once it's confirmed."

### What to know about the price

- The **Up** and **Down** prices add up to more than $1. The gap is the spread. It can widen when prices move fast and in a window's last 30 seconds.
- If no one is selling a side, its price shows "—". 1-tap reads "No one is selling Up right now — use Limit to bid for it".
- If you enter more than you can spend (your cash plus any trade credit), the buy shrinks to what you can afford.
- Immediate buys spend your [trade credit](/portfolio/trade-credit) before your cash.

## Limit orders

A limit order names your price. It waits at that price until another trader's order meets it, you cancel it, or the window closes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to Limit">
    Open **Market ▾** and choose **Limit**. It works on the **Buy** and **Sell** tabs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set contracts and price">
    Pick a side. Enter **Contracts** (whole numbers) and a **Limit price** in cents, from 1¢ to 99¢. On **Buy**, the readout shows **Cost** (the most the order can cost, fee included) and **To win** ($1 per contract).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose whether it can trade now">
    **Submit as resting order only** is ticked by default, so the order only ever waits. If any part would trade right away, it's refused. Untick it to let whatever can trade at your price trade now. The rest waits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it">
    Tap **Buy Up · limit**. A green line confirms it: "Order placed · 10 resting at 45¢", or "Filled 5 at 52¢ · 5 resting" when part traded at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

While a buy waits, its cost (plus a small fee allowance) is held out of **Buying power**. A waiting sell holds the contracts it offers. Limit orders never use trade credit, and your own orders never fill against each other.

Limit orders get no **Trade executed** card. When a waiting order fills later, the fill shows in Live Positions, your Portfolio activity, and the feed. No notification is sent.

### See or cancel a limit order

1. On that market's page, open **Live Positions** → **Open orders**. Each row reads like "Buy Up 10 @ 45¢", with " · 2 filled" once part has filled.
2. Tap **Cancel**. The order leaves the list, and the cash it held returns to **Buying power** at once.

Orders placed from a trade dialog show on the market's page too. Every order still waiting when the window closes is cancelled automatically, and its held cash returns. Any part that filled is a normal position and settles as usual.

<Note>
While a market is paused, **Limit** can't be picked. The website has no time-in-force choice: every limit order lasts until it fills, you cancel it, or the window closes. Developers who need other order types can use the [Trade API](/api/introduction).
</Note>

## The trade dialog

Tapping **Up** or **Down** on a card, a price on the interval tabs, the docked bar on a phone, or a trade on the feed opens the trade dialog.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Desktop and tablet">
    A live chart of the window, with its **Target** pill and trade pins. Under it, the market name ("Bitcoin (BTC) 5 min") over "$107,234.12 to beat" and a countdown. Then the ticket, with **Buy** pinned in a footer.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Phone">
    A bottom sheet with no chart. Its header reads "BTC Up or Down 5 min", with the side ahead (**Up**, **Down**, or **Tied**) and a clock pill that drains as the window runs. Under it, **To beat** and **Current price**. Then the ticket, with **Buy** pinned to the bottom.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

**Open market page ↗** at the top goes to the full page and closes the dialog. Press Esc, tap ✕, or click outside to close it. Opened from the feed on a wider screen, the dialog also shows the trader you'd be copying. See [Profiles and social](/platform/profiles-and-social).

If the stock's market is closed, the dialog reads "Market closed" with the stock's hours, "Crypto markets stay open 24/7.", and **Close**.

## Confirmation

After a Market or 1-tap order fills, a **Trade executed** card replaces the ticket:

- **Buy:** "Bought $5.00 Up @ 52¢" (what you spent, at the average price) and "Pays $9.48 if Up wins". With trade credit, a line such as "$3.00 credit + $2.00 cash · you keep the profit".
- **Sell:** "Sold $4.90 Up @ 49¢" and "+$0.40 realized · settling…". With trade credit, "$X back as trade credit".
- **Partial fill:** an amber line says how much of your amount filled at the live quote. That's all that was on offer within your price.
- **Share trade** (buys only) opens a share card.
- The take box: "Add your take", placeholder "Why this trade?", with **GIF**, **Skip**, and **Post**. Once posted: "Take posted — it's on your share card", then **Share trade** and **Done**.

**Skip** closes the card, and in the trade dialog closes the dialog too. See [Profiles and social](/platform/profiles-and-social) for takes.

## Selling back

You can sell before the window closes. The fastest way is **Live Positions**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="From Live Positions">
    Tap **Sell** on a side's row. It sells that side's whole position at the current bid in one tap, with no confirmation step. While a sale is in flight, every **Sell** button reads "Selling…". The row then updates or disappears.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From the ticket">
    Switch to the **Sell** tab. **Market** sells a dollar amount ("Sell value"), and the readout shows the contracts it sells. **Limit** offers whole contracts at your price.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

A sale moves no money at once. The difference between what you sold for and what you paid is settled within about a minute, and the card shows "· settling…" meanwhile. You can't sell more than you hold.

If trade credit paid for the position, a sale at a loss refills your credit first, and a sale at a profit uses it up. Live Positions previews the split before you sell. See [Trade credit](/portfolio/trade-credit).

<Tip>
Selling early gets you the current bid, not the $1 a winning contract pays at the close. Holding pays more if your side wins. Selling removes the uncertainty.
</Tip>

## Live Positions

On the market page, **Live Positions** sits under the ticket (under the chart on a phone).

| Element | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| "Buying power $X" (+ "+ $Y credit") | What you can spend now. The credit figure shows while you hold trade credit. |
| Row: "Up 3.00 contracts $1.62" and **Sell** | What you hold, valued at what selling it now would bring |
| "entry 51¢ · now 54¢" · "pays $3.00 if Up" | Your average entry, the current price to buy that side, and the payout if it wins |
| **Value** / **Unrealized** | The rows added up, and your gain or loss against what you paid |
| **Open orders** … **Cancel** | Your waiting limit orders in this window |
| **Total return** | Your settled results on this market |

Multiplier orders list first, with their own **Sell** button. With nothing held it reads "No position in this window yet."

<Note>
Live Positions values each side at what selling it now would bring, after the selling fee. Portfolio values it at the midpoint. So a fresh buy looks slightly negative here and roughly flat on Portfolio. The two meet when you sell or the window settles.
</Note>

## The Multiplier

On stock and crypto markets, a **Multiplier** slider sits at the bottom of the Buy ticket. At 1× nothing changes. Slide it up for a bigger payout on a bigger move. It shows only on **Buy** with **Market**, not on **Sell** or **Limit**. Above 1×, 1-tap boxes switch back to the amount box. See [Multiplier](/interval/multiplier).

## Fees, price protection, and sizes

- **Fees.** Orders that trade right away pay a small taker fee, built into what you spend and what **To win** shows. Limit orders that wait and are filled later pay no fee. Nothing is charged at settlement.
- **Price protection.** A buy won't fill far above the price you saw, and a sell won't fill far below it. This covers Market and 1-tap orders and Live Positions **Sell**. If nothing fills inside that range, the order is refused.
- **Prices** move in 1¢ steps, from 1¢ to 99¢.
- **Contracts** can be fractional, in steps of 0.01. Website limit orders take whole contracts.
- **No minimum** beyond a positive amount. An amount too small to buy anything is refused.
- **Time-out.** If an order gets no answer within 15 seconds, the ticket tells you it may still go through.
