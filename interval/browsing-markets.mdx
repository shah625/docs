When you open updown, you land on **Bitcoin's 5-minute market**. On a phone, the **Trade** tab opens the same market. To see every market at once, use the **Browse markets** page or a category page. For how a window works, see [How windows work](/interval/how-windows-work).

## What's listed

| Category | Markets | Intervals |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| **Stocks** | AAPL, NVDA, SPCX, TSLA, GOOGL, PLTR, META, HOOD, SPY, COIN | **5 min**, **15 min**, **60 min** |
| **Crypto** | BTC, ETH, SOL, DOGE, HYPE, XRP | **5 min**, **15 min**, **60 min** |
| **Pre-IPO** | OpenAI, Anthropic | **5 min**, **15 min**, **60 min**. See [Pre-IPO markets](/interval/pre-ipo). |

The list can change without notice. A market shows only while it can be traded:

- Stock markets leave every list outside their trading hours. PLTR and SPY leave from Friday 4:00 PM to Sunday 8:00 PM ET. See [How windows work](/interval/how-windows-work) for the hours.
- At times a whole interval can be paused for a category. Its markets then leave the lists until it reopens.

## Browse markets

**Browse markets** lists every market as a compact card. It has no menu entry. Reach it in any of these ways:

- Tap a **Browse markets** button, for example on your Portfolio when you have no open positions.
- Tap **Back to markets** on a closed stock market's page.
- Go to `updown.fast/trade`.

The sub-line counts what's showing, for example "All Categories · 15 min · N markets".

<Steps>
  <Step title="Filter">
    Use the category dropdown (**All Categories**, **Stocks**, **Crypto**, **Pre-IPO**) and the interval dropdown (**All Intervals**, **5 min**, **15 min**, **60 min**). Each item shows its count. The page opens on **All Categories · 15 min**. Pick **Pre-IPO** to see the pre-IPO markets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Trade from a card, or open it">
    Tap **▲ Up** or **▼ Down** on a card to open the trade dialog with that side picked. Tap anywhere else on the card to open [the market page](/interval/the-market-page).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or place several at once">
    Turn on **Bundle** to pick a side on up to 20 markets and place them in one order. See [Bundles](/interval/bundles).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your filters are kept in the page's URL, so you can bookmark a view. Under **All Intervals**, a market's intervals sit side by side. Under **All Categories**, the most volatile market comes first, and markets that haven't opened yet come last.

### The compact card

| Part | What it shows |
|------|--------------|
| **Image and title** | The market, for example "Bitcoin (BTC)" |
| **To beat** \| **Now** | The price to beat, then the current price in green (above) or red (below), with the move, such as "▲ 0.42". Before the window opens, **To beat** reads "—". |
| **Up** / **Down** | "▲ Up 51¢" and "▼ Down 49¢", with live prices. On a phone they stack at the right edge as "▲ 51¢" / "▼ 49¢". A side with no price shows "—". |
| **Footer** | The interval ("15 min") and a countdown. The countdown is white, turns amber under half the window, and red in the last fifth. Before kickoff it reads "Opens 2:30 PM". |

If nothing can be listed at any interval, the page reads "No markets right now" and "New recurring markets roll out here as their metrics go live."

## The Stocks, Crypto, and Pre-IPO pages

Each category has its own page: `updown.fast/stocks`, `updown.fast/crypto`, and `updown.fast/pre-ipo`. They have no menu entry, so open them by URL. Each page has:

- An interval tab row, **5 min · 15 min · 60 min**, with counts. It offers only the intervals the category runs right now. The default is **15 min**.
- A **Bundle** toggle beside the tabs.
- The **featured panel**, showing the category's first live market, such as Apple for Stocks or Bitcoin for Crypto.
- The rest of the category as **live cards**: one column on a phone, two from tablet width.

### The featured panel

| Element | What it shows |
|---------|--------------|
| **Title** | The market and interval, "Bitcoin (BTC) · 15 min". Stocks add a session pill: **Market hours**, **Extended hours**, or **Overnight**. |
| **Market / Pays out / Odds** | One row per side, with a probability bar, the payout multiple, and a price button: "▲ Up · 1.96x · 51¢". The price button opens the trade dialog. |
| **Closes in** / **Opens in** | A countdown to the close, or to the open before kickoff |
| **To beat** and **Now** | The price to beat (with the close time) and the current price (with the move, "+$0.42 (+0.07%)") |
| **Chart** | The last 5 minutes, rolling, with a **Target** pill at the price to beat and the avatars of traders where they traded |

<Note>
The "Pays out" multiple is 1 divided by the price. It ignores the spread and the fee you pay when your order fills.
</Note>

The session pill appears on the featured panel and on the market page, not on the compact cards. Pre-IPO markets show no session pill.

### Live cards

| Part | What it shows |
|------|--------------|
| **Header** | Image, title, the interval, and a countdown (or "Opens 2:30 PM") |
| **To beat** | The price, with the close time ("2:30 PM close") |
| **Now** | The current price in green or red, with the move |
| **Sides** | An **▲ Up** row and a **▼ Down** row, each with a price button and a probability bar. Before kickoff: **Trading opens in** with a countdown. With no price yet: "Waiting for the first quote". |
| **Footer** | **Live** (with a red dot) or **Upcoming**, and a **Last 6** strip of ▲ / ▼ results, oldest first |

Hover a result triangle to see the window, for example "2:00 PM window · $325.40 → $325.62 · Up". A window that closed unchanged adds " (unchanged)". The price button opens the trade dialog. The rest of the card's top half opens the market page.

If a category has nothing to list, its page says so, for example "No stock markets right now" and "Stock markets list while their price feed is live.", with a **Browse all markets** button.

## Other ways to find a market

- **Search:** press **Ctrl/⌘ + K** and type a name or ticker. Each result has **5 min**, **15 min**, and **60 min** chips.
- **The side panel:** on wide screens, its **Trade** tab lists markets under **Trending**, **Stocks**, **Crypto**, and **Pre-IPO**. A row opens that market's 15-minute page.
- **On a phone:** the **Home** tab opens on a **Trade** list with the same sections.

See [Navigating updown](/platform/navigating-updown) for both.

## Freshness

Card prices update live. The market list refreshes every minute, so stock cards drop out and come back at the edges of their trading hours on their own.
