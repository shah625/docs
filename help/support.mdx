## Contact us

Click **Contact us** in the status bar at the bottom of the screen on desktop, or tap ☰ → **More** → **Contact us** on a phone. The page reads "Questions, feedback, or partnership inquiries — we'd love to hear from you." Pick a channel:

| Channel | Where |
|---------|-------|
| **Email** | [hello@updown.fast](mailto:hello@updown.fast) |
| **X** | [@tradeupdown](https://x.com/tradeupdown) |
| **Discord** | [Join our server](https://discord.gg/d9gfrYWSAu) |

The Discord and X icons at the end of the status bar, and in ☰ → **More**, open the same places.

## What to include

- The **transaction hash** for a deposit or withdrawal question. Deposits arrive on Robinhood Chain, Arbitrum, Base, Ethereum, or Polygon, and withdrawals go out on Robinhood Chain. You can check each on its network's block explorer.
- The **market and window time** for an interval question. Every settled window has its own page. Open it from the **Past** dropdown on the market page, or from your activity.

## Restricted accounts

updown restricts an account linked to activity that breaks the [Terms of Service](https://www.updown.fast/terms). You then see an **Account flagged** notice.

- A restricted account can't trade, deposit, or earn rewards.
- Your balance isn't affected. You can still withdraw at any time.
- Anything else you try is refused with a reminder that you can still withdraw.
- The notice comes back on every page until you tick **Don't show this again** and click **Got it**.

If you think it's a mistake, contact us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/d9gfrYWSAu) or at [hello@updown.fast](mailto:hello@updown.fast), and we'll take a look.

## Mis-sent deposits

Your deposit address is the same on every network, but only these are credited:

| Network | Asset |
|---------|-------|
| **Robinhood Chain** | USDG |
| **Arbitrum**, **Base**, **Polygon** | USDC |
| **Ethereum** | USDC or USDT |

Other tokens are credited only when you send them through **Transfer any crypto** in **Deposit with**, which runs through our payment partner.

A supported deposit below its network's minimum waits. It's added to your balance once you top it up past the minimum. See [Deposit and withdraw](/portfolio/deposit-withdraw).

These aren't credited, and there's no automated recovery:

- an unsupported token, including bridged USDC.e on Polygon;
- a network marked "Soon", such as BNB Chain, even though the same address works there;
- a wrong address.

<Warning>
As the Terms put it: "Check every withdrawal address carefully: blockchain transfers cannot be reversed, and we cannot recover funds sent to a wrong address." If something went to the wrong place, email [hello@updown.fast](mailto:hello@updown.fast) with the transaction hash as soon as you can.
</Warning>

## Trade errors and disputes

Email [hello@updown.fast](mailto:hello@updown.fast) for both.

- **A suspected pricing error:** report it "where possible within 60 minutes of the trade and before the market settles". See [Terms section 9](https://www.updown.fast/terms#errors).
- **A formal dispute:** put "Dispute" in the subject line. See [Terms section 23](https://www.updown.fast/terms#disputes).

## Availability

The Terms of Service require you to be 18 or older and not located in a Restricted Jurisdiction (listed in the [Terms](https://www.updown.fast/terms#restricted)). See [Legal and eligibility](/help/legal-and-eligibility).

## Cookie choices

To change your cookie choice, click **Your privacy choices** in the status bar, or tap ☰ → **More** → **Your privacy choices** on a phone. The cookie banner opens again. Click **Decline** or **Accept**. See [Cookies and privacy choices](/help/legal-and-eligibility#cookies-and-privacy-choices).

## Service status

On desktop, the status bar shows an **Online** / **Offline** dot for whether the app can reach updown. There's no separate status page.

When updown pauses trading, a banner appears at the top of every page:

> Trading is paused for maintenance. Resuming in 1h 30m. Join our Discord for additional updates

- The message can differ, and "Resuming in…" shows only when a resume time is set.
- The banner is usually amber, or blue for information. It scrolls away with the page.
- Every ticket shows the message too, such as "Trading is paused for maintenance. Resumes in 1h 30m."
- The pause lifts on its own at the resume time, and the banner disappears.
- A pause can stop new positions only, all trading, or all money movement: deposits, withdrawals, and reward claims. Settlement keeps running either way.
- Every banner links to our Discord, where updates are posted.

During a short platform upgrade, tickets may instead read "We're upgrading the platform — trading and transfers are paused for a few minutes." with no banner.

updown can also pause a single interval in one category, with no banner. Its markets drop out of the lists. If you open one from a link, the ticket is covered by a **Trading paused** note. You can still sell, and positions you hold settle as usual.
