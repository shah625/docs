---
title: "Glossary"
description: "Key terms used across updown."
---

## 0–9

**1-tap**
A switch on the ticket's **Buy** tab that turns the amount boxes ($1 / $5 / $10 / $25) into instant buys. Off by default.

**60-second value**
How a window's open and close are set. Stocks use the simple average of 60 one-second prices. Crypto uses a 60-second time-weighted average. Pre-IPO uses the median of 60 seconds of samples. A window's close is also the next window's **To beat**.

## A

**Account flagged**
The notice on a restricted account. A restricted account can't trade, deposit, or earn rewards, but it can always withdraw its balance.

**Activity**
The table on your Portfolio page, and on public profiles, listing buys, sells, and settlements, with the filters **All activity**, **Trades**, and **Takes**. Deposits and withdrawals aren't listed.

**Allow trading**
The one-time approval, in a panel headed "Enable trading". It lets updown sign the transactions that carry out your trades, settle them, pay you out, and process the withdrawals you request, so you don't sign each trade. Your balance stays in your wallet. You need it to trade, to see the stablecoin deposit address, and to withdraw.

## B

**Break-even**
For a multiplier order, the closing price at which it pays back its stake. While the slider is above 1×, the market's **To beat** shows it in purple. Once the order is placed, the chart marks it as "Your break-even".

**Browse markets**
The page listing every interval market as cards, filtered by category and interval. It opens on 15 min, at `updown.fast/trade`.

**Bundle**
Several interval buys placed together with one button: up to 20, one pick per market. Each pick is its own buy and wins or loses on its own. Formerly called "Multi-buy".

**Buying power**
The **Live Positions** figure on a market page. It's the same as **Cash**, with your trade credit shown beside it.

## C

**Cash**
Money free to trade: your USDG balance less what your open positions and resting limit buys commit. Shown in the top bar. Click it to deposit. Trade credit isn't included.

**Category page**
The Stocks, Crypto, and Pre-IPO pages. Each shows a featured market, then the rest of the category as cards.

**Chance**
The ticket's probability for a side: the midpoint between the price to buy it and the price to sell it.

**Chance of profit**
An estimate of the chance a multiplier order pays back more than its stake.

**Committed**
Cash held for contracts you hold in open windows, for resting limit buys, and for a sale at a loss until the loss is collected. It can't be withdrawn until those settle.

**Contract**
The unit you trade. An interval contract is one Up or Down claim on a window, priced 1¢–99¢, that pays $1.00 if its side wins. Quantities come in steps of 0.01 contract ("3.00 contracts"). Limit orders on the website use whole contracts.

**Copy / Copy trade**
Opens the same market and side in the window that's open now, ready to buy. It shows on buys with a take, on feed posts on a phone and in a replay. In the desktop feed, clicking the trade does the same.

**Copy bundle**
Puts every still-trading pick of someone's Bundle into your Bundle slip.

**Covered trade**
A trade-credit promotion, when offered. For accounts created while it runs, a first trade that loses is refunded in trade credit, up to a cap.

**Creator rewards**
Points you earn for social activity: takes, GIFs, likes, follows, followers, and copies of your trades. You claim them as trade credit. Only you see them.

**Credit**
The top-bar figure for your unused trade credit, shown only while you have some.

## D

**Deposit tracker**
Follows a card, Cash App, or other payment-partner deposit on its way.

**Deposit with**
The deposit menu. Its **Cash** tab offers card and Cash App. Its **Crypto** tab offers stablecoins, any crypto, wallets, and exchanges.

**Division**
A step within an ELO tier, from 1 (lowest) to 3 (highest), for example "Gold 2".

**Down**
The side that wins if the close is strictly below the price to beat. An unchanged close settles **Up**.

## E

**ELO**
Your trader rating: how well you beat the prices you pay. It starts at 100 (Bronze 3) and moves when your interval positions settle or are sold. Long-shot wins move it most, and your first trades move it the most. P&L, volume, and takes add bounded credit. Your rating, tier, and record are public; the breakdown is shown only to you.

**ELO change board**
The ELO board over **24H**, **7D**, or **30D**, ranked by rating gained.

## F

**Feed**
The live list of everyone's trades. On desktop it's the side panel's **Feed** tab. On a phone, it's **Home** → **Feed**.

**Final**
A window's settlement value, shown after the close.

**First-deposit match**
A trade-credit promotion, when offered. Your first deposit is matched dollar for dollar in trade credit, up to a cap.

## G

**Global / Following / This market**
The feed's tabs: everyone's trades; trades by people you follow; trades on this asset's markets.

**Go to Live**
The button that leaves an ended window for the current one.

## H

**Home**
The phone tab that opens your balance, **Deposit**, and the **Trade · Feed · Leaderboard** tabs.

**Hot take**
A take on a long shot, meaning an interval buy under 20¢. It gets a 🔥 badge on the feed.

## I

**In positions**
The current value of your open positions, shown on the Portfolio value card as "$X cash · $Y in positions".

**Interval market**
An Up or Down market on a stock, crypto, or Pre-IPO price, over 5-, 15-, or 60-minute windows.

**Interval switcher**
The row on a market page with the same subject's other intervals (5 min · 15 min · 60 min), each with its countdown and prices.

## L

**Last 4**
A market's last four settled windows, shown as ▲ / ▼ chips in the phone's market lists.

**Last 5**
The strip on a market page with its last five settled results.

**Leaderboard**
The side panel's tab with the **PNL** and **ELO** boards: the top 20 over **24H**, **7D**, **30D**, or **ALL**. **Full board →** opens the full ELO board. On a phone, it's **Home** → **Leaderboard**.

**Like**
A heart on any trade. It notifies the trader.

**Limit order**
An order to buy or sell a set number of contracts at a price you choose. It waits until another trader's order meets that price, you cancel it, or the window closes. A limit order that waits and is filled later pays no fee.

**LIVE**
The chart view of the last 60 seconds, scrolling with the clock. The default on 5- and 15-minute markets.

**Live position**
The feed's hover card for a trade whose window is still open: P&L so far, what it's worth if sold now, the chance, and the time left.

**Live Positions**
The market-page panel with your contracts and multiplier orders in the current window, their **Sell** buttons, your **Buying power**, and your **Open orders**.

## M

**Market**
An interval subject at one interval, for example "Apple (AAPL) · 15 min". Also the ticket's default order type, which spends a dollar amount at the live quote.

**Market Rules**
The rules block for each window: the **Target Price**, "Resolves ▲ Up if …", the full rules, and **Timeline and payout**. It's under the market page's **Rules** tab.

**MKT**
The chart view of the whole current window from its open. The default on 60-minute markets.

**Multi-buy**
The old name for **Bundle**.

**Multiplier**
A slider on the interval **Buy** ticket, from 1× to 10×, on stock and crypto interval markets. At 1× the order is a regular Up or Down contract. Above 1× it pays more the further the price closes your way, up to a cap, and less, possibly nothing, on a small move. The most you can lose is your stake.

**Multiplier order**
An order placed with the slider above 1×, shown as "2.4× multiplier · $5.00 stake".

## N

**Notifications**
The bell at the top right. It lists your newest 50 notices, such as settlements, follows, likes, copies, withdrawals, trade credit, and Team changes.

**Now**
An interval market's live price. On stocks and crypto it's a rolling 60-second average, so it tracks the value the window settles on.

## O

**Open market page**
The trade dialog's link to the full market page.

**Open orders**
Your waiting limit orders in the current window, listed in **Live Positions** with **Cancel**.

## P

**Partial fill**
An order that filled for less than the amount you entered, because not enough was on offer within your price protection, or because the amount was more than your **Buying power** and trade credit together.

**Past**
The market page's menu of recently settled windows. Each opens its settled-window page.

**Pays**
On an interval position, what you receive if your side wins: $1.00 per contract held.

**Points (pts)**
Creator-reward units. 1 pt = 1¢, and every 500 pts converts to $5.00 of trade credit.

**Portfolio**
The top-bar figure: your cash plus your open positions at their current value (a settling window at its cost). Also the page with your profile, balance, positions, and activity.

**Portfolio value**
The Portfolio page's figure, shown with "$X cash · $Y in positions".

**Pre-IPO market**
An interval market on a private company, such as OpenAI or Anthropic. It follows a third-party pre-IPO market whose price tracks the company's implied valuation ($1 of price = $1 billion). Cash-settled; you own no shares.

**Price protection**
A buy won't fill far above the price you saw, and a sell won't fill far below it. Otherwise: "Your order could not be filled…".

**Price tape**
The scrolling live prices in the desktop status bar. Each one opens its 15-minute market.

**Prize pool**
The prizes shared by a Trading Tournament's top three. The current one is $1,000: $600 / $300 / $100.

**Profit & loss chart**
The Portfolio value card's chart of cumulative P&L. It starts at $0 at the start of the selected range, so deposits and withdrawals never move it.

## R

**Realized P&L**
Profit or loss locked in on interval contracts, by selling or by settlement. Shown on your profile. Multiplier orders aren't included.

**Record**
Your wins and losses on scored results, for example "12–8".

**Refund**
What happens to a multiplier order whose window has no result within 6 hours of the close: the stake comes back.

**Regular contract**
A 1× Up or Down contract. It pays $1 if you're right, whatever the size of the move.

**Replay**
A short animation of a settled buy's window: the price against the level to beat, where the trader bought, and the P&L.

**Resting order only**
The limit ticket's "Submit as resting order only" box, ticked by default. While it's ticked, the order is refused if any part of it would trade at once, so it only ever waits.

**Restricted Jurisdiction**
A place where updown isn't offered. The Terms of Service list them under [Restricted jurisdictions](https://www.updown.fast/terms#restricted).

**Rewards bar**
The top-bar meter of your points toward your next $5.00 of trade credit.

**Robinhood Chain**
The network your wallet and cash are on. It's the default deposit network and the only withdrawal network.

**ROI**
In the Trading Tournament, your net P&L divided by the total cost of the buys behind it, as a percent.

**Routed deposit**
USDC on Arbitrum, Base, Ethereum, or Polygon, or USDT on Ethereum, moved automatically into your Robinhood Chain USDG. A small amount comes off when it's moved.

## S

**Search**
Finds interval markets on stocks, Pre-IPO companies, and crypto. Open it with **Search markets…** or Ctrl K / ⌘K.

**Sell \~$X**
The **Live Positions** button that sells a multiplier order before the close, at its current sell-back price.

**Selling back**
Selling contracts before the close, from **Live Positions** → **Sell** or the ticket's **Sell** tab. The realized difference is paid or collected within about a minute.

**Send stablecoins**
The deposit option for sending USDG, USDC, or USDT straight to your deposit address.

**Session pill**
The label on a stock market's page: **Market hours**, **Extended hours**, or **Overnight**.

**Settings**
The avatar-menu dialog with your profile fields, **Visibility**, **Marketing emails**, **Log out**, and **Delete account**.

**Settlement**
How a window pays out. It settles on its 60-second final value shortly after it closes: the result shows within seconds, and payouts usually land within a minute or two. Each winning contract pays $1.00.

**Settling…**
The brief state after a window closes, before its result is recorded.

**Share card**
The image made by **Share trade** or **Share picks** after a buy or a Bundle.

**Side panel**
The left column on wide screens, with the **Watchlist · Trade · Leaderboard · Feed** tabs. You can resize it.

**Sign-up bonus**
A trade-credit promotion, when offered: trade credit given when an account is created.

**Spread**
The gap between the price to buy a side and the price you'd get selling it. Up and Down buy prices add up to more than $1 by this gap. It can widen when prices move fast and in a window's last 30 seconds.

**Stake**
The dollars you put into a multiplier order, from $1 to $25. It's taken from your cash when you place the order.

**Suggested markets**
Three other markets shown beside or below a market page.

## T

**Take**
A note of up to 280 characters, a GIF, or both, posted with a trade. A Bundle shares one take. Takes show on the feed and in your Activity, and can't be edited.

**Taker fee**
Orders that trade right away pay a small taker fee, built into what you spend and what **To win** shows. Limit orders that wait and are filled later pay no fee. Nothing is charged at settlement.

**Target Price**
The price to beat, as **Market Rules** shows it.

**Team access**
Letting another updown account use yours, as **View only** or **Can trade**. Members can never withdraw or deposit.

**Terms of Service**
The rules for using updown, at [updown.fast/terms](https://www.updown.fast/terms). The **Privacy Policy** is at [updown.fast/privacy](https://www.updown.fast/privacy).

**Tier**
One of seven ELO bands. Every account starts in Bronze, then climbs through Silver, Gold, and up.

**Time left**
The countdown to a window's close. It turns from white to amber, then red.

**To beat**
The level a window must beat: the previous window's final 60-second value. It shows "—" until it's known. With the Multiplier above 1×, it shows your order's break-even in purple.

**To win**
What a buy pays if its side wins ($1 per contract), after the fee.

**Top traders**
The feed card of the six traders with the best 7-day P&L, among those in profit.

**Total return**
Your realized P&L on one market across its settled windows, shown in **Live Positions**.

**Total volume (Trading Tournament)**
The tournament's name for the total cost of your buys counted toward ROI. You need $50 of it to be ranked. It isn't the **Volume** stat on profiles.

**Trade (phone tab)**
Opens the Bitcoin · 5 min market.

**Trade credit**
A promotional balance, shown as **Credit**, spent before your cash on immediate interval buys. It can't be withdrawn, and the profit it makes is yours.

**Trade executed**
The confirmation card after a Market or 1-tap order fills, or after a multiplier order is placed.

**Trading paused**
A market or a group of markets temporarily closed to new positions. Its page stays readable, and open positions settle as usual.

**Trading Tournament**
A competition over a fixed period, with a prize pool. The current one runs Oct 22–28 and is ranked by ROI, with a $50 minimum.

**Trending**
The side panel's default market list: stocks, Pre-IPO, and crypto mixed, biggest move either way first.

## U

**Unrealized**
Current value minus cost of the contracts you still hold.

**Up**
The side that wins if the close is at or above the price to beat, both rounded to 4 decimal places (7 for DOGE). An unchanged close settles Up.

**USDC / USDT**
Stablecoins you can deposit on other networks: USDC on Arbitrum, Base, Ethereum, or Polygon, and USDT on Ethereum. They're moved into your USDG automatically.

**USDG**
The stablecoin your updown balance is held in, on Robinhood Chain. You deposit, trade, and withdraw in it.

## V

**Visibility**
The **Edit profile** switches that hide parts of your public profile (**Public** / **Hidden**): your balance and chart, your positions, and your activity.

**Volume**
$1 per interval contract bought or sold. Shown on your profile. Multiplier orders aren't included.

## W

**Watchlist**
Assets you've starred, in the side panel's **Watchlist** tab. Saved to your account and synced across devices when you're signed in.

**Weekend pricing**
From Friday 4 PM to Sunday 8 PM ET, windows on the weekend-trading stocks price off a 24/7 market that tracks where traders expect the stock to reopen.

**Win up to**
The most a multiplier order can pay, reached when the price closes at or past its cap. It includes the stake.

**Window**
One round of an interval market, with a fixed open and close: 5, 15, or 60 minutes.

**Won / Lost**
Activity labels for settled positions.

## Y

**Your privacy choices**
The link that reopens the cookie banner: in the desktop bottom bar, or under ☰ → **More** on a phone.
