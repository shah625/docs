Open them from **Terms** and **Privacy** in the status bar on desktop, or ☰ → **More** → **Terms of Service** / **Privacy Policy** on a phone. The sign-in popup links them too. They're public, so you can read them signed out.

To opt out of arbitration, email [hello@updown.fast](mailto:hello@updown.fast) within 30 days of first accepting the Terms.

Report a suspected pricing error within 60 minutes of the trade where possible. See [Trade errors and disputes](/help/support#trade-errors-and-disputes).

Questions about any of the documents go to [hello@updown.fast](mailto:hello@updown.fast).

## Who can use updown

The [Terms](https://www.updown.fast/terms#eligibility) set these rules:

| Rule | What the Terms say |
|------|--------------------|
| **Age** | You must "be at least 18 years old, and at least the age of majority where you live, and able to form a binding contract" |
| **Location** | You must "not be located in, or ordinarily resident in, a Restricted Jurisdiction". The list is in the [Terms](https://www.updown.fast/terms#restricted) |
| **Understanding** | You must "understand how the products work and their risks, including that you can lose everything you pay for a position" |
| **One account** | "You may hold only one account." You can let other people use it through [Team](/platform/team-access). You're responsible for what they do, and each of them must meet these rules and accept the Terms |

## Your money and trading permission

| Topic | What applies |
|-------|--------------|
| **Your balance** | Your cash is the USDG held in your wallet on Robinhood Chain. The wallet is created for you when you sign in, linked to your login. The Terms say "we do not hold it in our own accounts" |
| **Trading permission** | **Allow trading** lets updown sign transactions from your wallet to carry out your trades, settle them, pay you out, and process the withdrawals you request. See [Account and profile](/account-and-profile) |
| **Withdrawals** | You move money out by making a withdrawal in updown. Withdrawals are restricted only for one of these reasons: a pause of the whole service; a security incident; a requirement of law, a court, or a sanctions program; or a review of a possible erroneous trade or a breach of the Terms involving your account |
| **Not a bank** | "We are not a bank. Balances are not deposits and are not insured by any government or deposit insurance scheme." |
| **What updown is** | All products are cash-settled. "updown is not an exchange, a broker-dealer, a bank or an investment adviser." |

See [Deposit and withdraw](/portfolio/deposit-withdraw) for how money moves in and out.

## Accepting updated Terms

New accounts accept the Terms at sign-up. The sign-in popup reads "By logging in I agree to the Terms & Privacy Policy".

Some older accounts see a one-time prompt instead:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the prompt">
    A popup headed **"We’ve updated our Terms"** reads "By selecting Agree, you confirm you’re 18 or older and accept our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy." Both links open in a new tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the email question, if it's there">
    If you haven't answered it before, a checkbox above the buttons reads "Email me about new markets, promotions and product updates." Pressing **Agree** saves that answer too.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agree">
    Click **Agree**. If you haven't allowed trading yet and you opened the prompt by trying to trade, **Allow trading** opens next.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Not now** puts the prompt away for this browser session. It comes back when you try to buy. Until you agree, new trades are refused with "Please accept our updated Terms of Service to keep trading. Reload the page to see them."

<Note>
Without agreeing, you can still browse, sell positions you hold, and withdraw. Open positions settle as usual.
</Note>

## Cookies and privacy choices

Until you choose, a banner runs along the bottom of the screen:

> **Cookies.** We use cookies to keep you signed in and, with your OK, to understand how updown is used (including recordings of your visits, with what you type masked) and improve our marketing. Learn more

**Learn more** opens the cookies section of the [Privacy Policy](https://www.updown.fast/privacy#cookies). Click **Decline** or **Accept**:

| Choice | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Accept** | Turns on analytics cookies, recordings of your visits with typing masked, masked error replays, and advertising measurement |
| **Decline** | Turns those off. Visits are still counted, without cookies or local storage. Switching from **Accept** to **Decline** reloads the page |

Your choice lasts 1 year. If you accepted before 15 October 2026, the banner asks you again.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, the banner adds "Your browser’s Global Privacy Control signal is on, so ad tracking stays off whatever you choose."

To change your choice, click **Your privacy choices** in the status bar, or tap ☰ → **More** → **Your privacy choices** on a phone.

## Marketing emails

updown asks once whether you'd like emails, with the checkbox "Email me about new markets, promotions and product updates." It appears in the first popup you see after signing in. To change your answer any time, open **Settings** and use the **Marketing emails** switch under Emails. It saves at once.

## Delete your account

<Warning>
Withdraw your cash first. Money left in your wallet can't be recovered once the account is gone.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Open the avatar menu → **Settings**, or on a phone ☰ → **PROFILE** → **Settings**. The **Edit profile** pencil on your Portfolio opens the same dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the deletion">
    Next to "Permanently remove your account and profile.", click **Delete account**. A dialog headed "Delete your account?" opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Tick "I understand my account will be permanently deleted and this can't be undone." Then click **Delete account**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The dialog explains: "Your login, profile, username, takes and follows are removed for good. Withdraw your cash first — money left in your wallet can't be recovered once the account is gone. If anything is still in your wallet or open, we'll file a request instead and tell you what to do."

- **Deleted at once:** if your wallet is empty, nothing is open or settling, and no payout is on its way, you see "Your account was deleted" and you're signed out.
- **Deletion requested:** otherwise a request is filed, with one of these reasons:
  - "Your wallet still holds money. Withdraw it first — once the account is deleted it can't be recovered."
  - "You still have open positions or a payout on its way. Once they settle and you withdraw, try again."
  - "We couldn't confirm your wallet is empty right now. Try again in a few minutes."

After a request, the row reads "Deletion requested" with the date, and "Withdraw your cash and let open positions settle, then try again to delete it right away — or we'll finish it within 30 days." Click **Try again** when you're ready.

Deleting removes your login and wallet, hides and anonymizes your profile, and removes your takes, likes, and follows. Signing in again with the same email creates a new account.
