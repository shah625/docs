The **Portfolio** page is your balance sheet and your profile in one. On desktop, click **Portfolio** in the top bar. On a phone, tap the **Portfolio** tab.

Signed out, the page reads "Sign in to view your profile" / "Your cash, positions and history live here." with a **Sign in** button.

Your balance is **USDG** on **Robinhood Chain**, held in your wallet. Every figure on this page reads from that one balance.

## The top bar

On desktop, the top bar shows your money on every page:

| Figure | What it is |
|--------|-----------|
| **Cash** | Money free to trade: your balance less what your open positions and waiting limit orders hold. Tooltip "Cash = available to trade · click to deposit". Click it to open the deposit menu. |
| **Credit** | Your spendable [trade credit](/portfolio/trade-credit), in blue. It shows only while you have some. Click it to open Portfolio. |
| **Portfolio** | Your cash plus your open positions at their current value. Tooltip "Portfolio = cash + open positions". Click it to open Portfolio. |

The top bar refreshes every 30 seconds.

On a phone, the **Home** tab opens with a balance block. The big figure is your portfolio value. "$X cash to trade" is the same as **Cash**, and **Deposit** opens the deposit menu. Tap the big figure to open Portfolio.

## The page, top to bottom

1. **Header**: your ELO panel, avatar, "@name", and X link; the stats **Volume** and **Realized P&L**; a pencil icon ("Edit profile") and a sign-out icon ("Sign out"); and a footer with your followers, who you follow, "⇄ N trades", your email (only you see it), and "📅 Joined Mon YYYY". See [Leaderboards and ELO](/platform/leaderboards-and-elo).
2. **Value card**: your profit and loss over time and your balance. See [The value card](#the-value-card).
3. **Your positions**: the **Open** tab lists every open position and multiplier order. See [Positions and history](/portfolio/positions-and-history).
4. **Creator reward** card: your points and the **Claim** button. See [Creator reward card](#creator-reward-card).
5. **Activity**: your trade history. See [Positions and history](/portfolio/positions-and-history#activity).

On desktop, the value card and **Your positions** sit in the left column, with the Creator reward card and **Activity** on the right. On screens 1536 px and wider, a sticky right rail adds a **Deposit** / **Withdraw** panel and **Follow top traders**. On narrower screens, **Follow top traders** closes the page.

On a phone, the order is the header, the value card, the Creator reward card, then two tabs, **Positions** and **Activity**, then **Follow top traders**.

## The value card

| Element | What it shows |
|---------|--------------|
| **Hero** (for example "+$12.40") | Your profit and loss over the selected range, signed and coloured. It follows the cursor when you hover the chart. |
| "Profit & loss · 7d" | The range in words: 24h, 7d, 30d, 1y, or all time. |
| **1D / 1W / 1M / 1Y / ALL** | The range. The default is **1W**. |
| **Chart** | Your cumulative profit and loss over the range. Hover it to read the value at a date and time. |
| **Portfolio value** | Your total, with "$X cash · $Y in positions" beneath it. |
| **Withdraw** / **Deposit** | Open the [withdraw dialog](/portfolio/deposit-withdraw#withdraw) and the [deposit menu](/portfolio/deposit-withdraw#open-the-deposit-menu). |
| Trade-credit line | "$A trade credit · $B in positions · not withdrawable — the profit is". A is your spendable credit and B is credit in open positions. It's hidden when both are $0. |

How to read the chart:

- It starts at **$0 at the start of the range**. The dashed $0 line marks where the range began.
- It adds up your realized results from interval sales and settlements. Its last point also counts your open positions at their current value.
- The line is green when the range is up and red when it's down.
- Deposits and withdrawals never move it.
- A range never starts before your account was created.
- With no trades, it reads "No trades yet".
- It refreshes every 30 seconds.

## Creator reward card

Only you see this card. It shows:

- the label "Creator reward" and a **See how to earn** button;
- your points, and "500 pts = $5.00 of trade credit";
- a **Claim** button, which reads "Claim at 500 pts" until you have enough;
- a progress bar, and the line "N pts to go · credit is used before your cash, and you keep the profit".

Claimed points become trade credit. See [Creator rewards](/platform/creator-rewards) and [Trade credit](/portfolio/trade-credit).

## What each figure means

| Figure | Where | What it is |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| **Cash** | Top bar; "$X cash to trade" on a phone | Your USDG balance less what's held for open positions, waiting limit buys, and recent sales at a loss. Trade credit is never in it. See [What holds your funds](#what-holds-your-funds). |
| **Buying power** | **Live Positions** on a market page | The same as **Cash**. "+ $Y credit" appears beside it while you have trade credit. |
| **Portfolio** | Top bar | The same total as **Portfolio value**. |
| **Portfolio value** | Value card | "cash" plus "in positions". |
| "$X cash" | Value card | Your USDG balance less the cost of contracts you hold in interval windows, including windows that have closed but not yet settled. Cash held for a waiting limit buy still counts here, so this figure can be higher than **Cash**. |
| "$Y in positions" | Value card | Your interval contracts at the midpoint of the current bid and ask (at cost while a window settles), plus your multiplier orders at their current value (the stake when there's none). |
| **Credit** | Top bar; the trade-credit line | Your spendable trade credit. It counts in neither **Cash** nor **Portfolio**. |
| **Volume** | Header | $1 per interval contract on every buy and every sale. |
| **Realized P&L** | Header | Your interval sales and settlements, as the cash result. A loss funded by trade credit counts only the cash you lost. |

Multiplier orders don't count toward **Volume** or **Realized P&L**.

## What holds your funds

These count against **Cash**, **Buying power**, and what you can withdraw:

1. the cash cost of contracts you hold in an interval window, from the buy until that window settles;
2. waiting limit buys, and orders still being placed;
3. a sale at a loss, until the loss is collected (within about a minute).

These don't hold funds:

- a multiplier order's stake, because it leaves your cash when you place the order;
- trade credit, because it's never in your balance.

<Warning>
Funds held by open positions can't be withdrawn until those positions settle. A withdrawal that dips into them is refused, and the message tells you how much is committed.
</Warning>

## How money moves

- **Interval buys** move no money when you buy. The cost is held from your **Cash** until the window settles.
- **Settlement** is one net transfer per window, after the close. A win is paid into your balance. A loss is taken from it.
- **Selling before the close** moves nothing at the moment of the sale. What the sale realized is paid or collected within about a minute. Until then the confirmation card reads "+$X realized · settling…".
- **Multiplier orders** take the stake from your **Cash** when you place them, and pay out into **Cash** at settlement. They use cash only, never trade credit. **Portfolio value** counts open multiplier orders at their live value. See [Multiplier](/interval/multiplier).
- **Trade credit** is spent before your cash on immediate interval buys. See [Trade credit](/portfolio/trade-credit).
- **Deposits and withdrawals** change your **Cash**, but never the profit and loss chart. They don't appear in **Activity**. A notification confirms each withdrawal. See [Notifications](/platform/notifications).

<Note>
Positions refresh every 10 seconds. The top bar and the chart refresh every 30 seconds.
</Note>

## Your public profile

Other people see your page at `/u/your-username`. It shows your header, value card, positions, and Activity, subject to the **Visibility** switches in **Edit profile**. It never shows your email, a wallet address, your deposits or withdrawals, or your waiting orders. See [Account and profile](/account-and-profile) and [Profiles and social](/platform/profiles-and-social).
