Your open positions and your history live on the **Portfolio** page. Each interval market page also has a **Live Positions** panel for the window on screen. That's where you sell back and cancel waiting orders.

## Open positions

**Portfolio → Your positions → Open** lists every open interval position and multiplier order together, sorted by close time. The tab shows a count. On a phone, tap the **Positions** tab under the value card.

With nothing open, it reads "No open positions", with a **Browse markets** button.

Positions refresh every 10 seconds. Contracts show two decimals ("1.00 contracts").

### Interval rows

| Element | What it shows |
|---------|--------------|
| **Title** | The market image and title |
| **Status line** | "▲ 15m · closes in 12m", or "15m · settling" once the window has closed |
| **Detail line** | "3.00 contracts · 51¢ → 53¢ · pays $3.00 if up": your contracts, your entry price → the current price ("—" when there's none), and what they pay if your side wins |
| **Value and P&L** | The position's current value, with the P&L and percent beneath it ("+$0.06 (+2.0%)"), or "awaiting settle" after the close |
| **Button** | **Trade** opens the market page while the window is live. **View** opens the window once it's settling. A **Settles at close** chip shows instead when the market has no page. |

Portfolio values a position at the midpoint of the current bid and ask. **Live Positions** shows what selling would bring, so the two figures can differ.

### Multiplier rows

| Element | What it shows |
|---------|--------------|
| **Title** | The market title |
| **Status line** | ▲ or ▼, then "15 min · closes in 12m", or "15 min · settling" |
| **Detail line** | "2.4× multiplier · $5.00 stake · wins up to $23.10 · pays off the close" |
| **Value and P&L** | The order's live value, with the P&L against the stake beneath it ("+$0.20 (+4.0%)"), or "awaiting settle". While a sale goes through, the value is what the sale pays. With no live value, it shows the stake. |
| **Button** | **Trade** while the window is live, **View** once it's settling |

You sell a multiplier order from **Live Positions**, not from Portfolio. See [Multiplier](/interval/multiplier).

## Live Positions on a market page

On screens 1024 px and wider, **Live Positions** sits in the right rail. On narrower screens, it's under the chart. Click the chevron beside the title to collapse it.

| Element | What it shows |
|---------|--------------|
| **Buying power $X** | Your **Cash**: what you can spend now. "+ $Y credit" appears beside it while you have trade credit (hover: "Trade credit is used before your cash; you keep the profit it makes"). |
| **Multiplier orders** | Listed first, each with a **Sell ~$X** button. See [Multiplier](/interval/multiplier). |
| **A row per side you hold** | A coloured dot, "Up", "3.00 contracts", the value ("$1.62"), and a red **Sell** button. The sub-line reads "entry 51¢ · now 54¢" · "pays $3.00 if Up". |
| **Trade-credit line** | When trade credit funds the position: "Sell ≈ $X back as credit · $Y to wallet", or "Sell ≈ $X credit used (profit) · $Y to wallet" |
| **Value** / **Unrealized** | This window's totals: "Value $X" and "Unrealized +$X (+Y%)" |
| **Total return** | Your result on this market across settled windows. It shows once you've traded the market. |
| **Open orders** | Your waiting limit orders in this window, each with **Cancel** |

- A side's value is what selling the whole side would bring now, after the fee. **Value** and **Unrealized** use it too.
- "now 54¢" is the current price to buy that side.
- The **Value**, **Unrealized**, and **Total return** totals don't include multiplier orders.
- With nothing held, the panel reads "No position in this window yet."
- When sales are paused, a pause note appears in the panel.
- Positions refresh every 10 seconds, prices every 1.5 seconds, and open orders every 5 seconds.

### Open orders

Each waiting limit order has a row: a side dot, "Buy Up" or "Sell Up", then "10 @ 45¢". Once part of it fills, " · 2 filled" is added. The list covers this market's current window only. Limit orders you placed from a trade dialog show here too.

- **Cancel** takes one tap, with no confirmation step. The order leaves the list, and the cash a buy held returns to **Buying power** at once.
- At the window's close, every order still waiting is cancelled automatically, and its held cash returns. Any part that already filled is a normal position and settles with the window.

This panel is the one place to see and cancel your limit orders. See [Placing orders](/interval/placing-orders).

## Selling before the close

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Sell">
    In **Live Positions**, tap **Sell** on a side's row. It sells that side's whole position in one tap, at the current bid, with no confirmation step. It won't fill far below the current bid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the row to update">
    While a sale goes through, every **Sell** button reads "Selling…". Then the row updates, or disappears if you've sold everything.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To sell part of a position, use the ticket's **Sell** tab. **Market** sells by dollars ("Sell value") and shows the contracts you sell ("You sell 9.80 contracts"). **Limit** offers whole contracts at your price. See [Placing orders](/interval/placing-orders).

After a **Market** sale, the "Trade executed" card reads, for example, "Sold **$4.90** Up @ 49¢" (after the fee) and "+$0.40 realized · settling…". Nothing moves at the moment of the sale. The difference between what the sale brought and what you paid is paid or collected within about a minute.

- You can't sell more than you hold: "You don't hold that many contracts to sell."
- Trade credit behind a position comes back as credit on a sale at a loss, and is used up on a sale at a profit. See [Trade credit](/portfolio/trade-credit).

<Tip>
Selling early gets you the current bid instead of the $1 a winning contract pays at settlement. Holding to the close pays more if your side wins, but selling removes the uncertainty.
</Tip>

## Activity

**Activity** sits in the Portfolio page's right column. On a phone, tap the **Activity** tab under the value card.

- **Filters:** **All activity** · **Trades** (buys and sales only) · **Takes** (buys you posted with a take).
- **Columns:** **Market** · **Action** · **Amount** · **P&L** · **Time**. On a phone, each row is a two-line card.

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|--------------|
| **Market** | The logo, the asset name, and the cadence ("Bitcoin (BTC) 15 min"), with a detail line underneath. A multiplier order adds a stamp, such as "10×". |
| **Action** | A chip, and a ▲ or ▼ for the direction |
| **Amount** | Signed cash ("+$9.80" / "−$5.00") |
| **P&L** | The result with percent ("+$4.80 (+96%)"), or "—" |
| **Time** | Relative ("now", "12m", "3h", "5d"), then a short date after 30 days. Hover for the full date and time. |

### Interval rows

| Chip | Detail line |
|------|------------|
| **Buy** | "3.00 contracts @ 51¢" |
| **Sell** | A sale before the close, with what it brought and its P&L |
| **Won** | "3.00 contracts · paid $1 each" |
| **Lost** | "· settled worthless" |
| **Settled** | "· nothing owed either way": the window left nothing to pay or collect |
| **Take** | A blue chip on a buy you posted with a take. The row shows the quote, the GIF, and "♥ N likes". |

### Multiplier rows

A multiplier order gets a **Buy** row when you place it, and one result row.

| Event | Chip | Detail line | Amount | P&L |
|-------|------|------------|--------|-----|
| Placed | **Buy** | "$5.00 stake" | −$5.00 | — |
| Sold early | **Sell** | "$5.00 stake · sold for $6.12" | +$6.12 | +$1.12 |
| Paid out | **Won** | "$5.00 stake · paid $8.40" | +$8.40 | The payout less the stake |
| Settled worthless | **Lost** | "$5.00 stake · settled worthless" | $0.00 | −$5.00 |
| Refunded | **Settled** | "$5.00 stake · refunded" | +$5.00 | $0.00 |

Multiplier results don't count toward your **Volume** or **Realized P&L** stats. Each finished multiplier order also sends a notification: "Multiplier won $X" (the profit), "Multiplier paid $X" (back, but less than the stake), "Multiplier lost $X", "Multiplier refunded: $X", or "Multiplier sold for $X (+$Y)".

### How Activity groups rows

- A window you sold out of completely shows only its **Sell** rows. Any rounding cent lands on the last sale.
- A window where you held both sides shows the winning side.
- Each row opens that window's settled page.
- You see 25 rows, then **Show more** · N older. History reaches back to your latest 200 interval events, plus up to 200 multiplier events.
- Very old accounts may see a few rows with labels from earlier products.

Empty states read "No activity yet" / "Trades and settlements will show up here." (with **Browse markets**), "No trades yet", or "No takes yet" / "Trades posted with a take or a GIF land here."

<Note>
Deposits and withdrawals aren't listed in Activity, on your page or a public one. The "Withdrew $X" notification is the record of a withdrawal. See [Notifications](/platform/notifications).
</Note>

## Settled-window pages

Every window has its own page once it's settled. Open one from:

- the **Past** menu or a result pill on a market page;
- a row in **Activity**;
- a settlement notification.

The page, top to bottom:

1. **Header:** a back arrow, the market art, the market name and cadence (for example "Bitcoin (BTC)", "15 min Up or Down"), the date and window ("October 21 · 2:00 PM – 2:15 PM"), and a **Settled** chip. A window that hasn't closed shows **Live** instead.
2. **Result:** **To beat** and **Final** on the left. On the right, the window time over "Up Won" or "Down Won", or "Settling…" until the result is in.
3. **Chart:** the recorded window, with a "Target $X" pill for the price to beat and a "CLOSE $X" pill in the result's colour.
4. **Under the chart:** "This window has ended." · **Past ▾** · **Go to Live**. **Go to Live** opens the market's current window.
5. **Market Rules** for that window.

On desktop, the right rail shows "This window has ended." with **Go to Live**, then **Other intervals** (the same market at its other cadences) and similar markets.

The page shows the window's result, not your payout. Your result is in **Activity**. For how a window's result is decided, see [How windows work](/interval/how-windows-work).

If a window still isn't recorded 5 minutes after its close, the page reads "This window isn't recorded", with a **Back to the live market** link.
