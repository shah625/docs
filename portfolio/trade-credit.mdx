**Trade credit** is a promotional balance beside your cash, shown in blue. It's spent **before** your cash on immediate interval buys. The profit it makes is yours. The credit itself can't be withdrawn.

As the app puts it: "Credit is used on trades first — the profit is yours to withdraw."

## The rules

- Trade credit can only be used to trade on updown.
- It can't be withdrawn or transferred, and it has no cash value.
- Profit you earn on a trade funded with trade credit is yours.
- It counts in neither **Cash** nor **Portfolio**. The top bar shows it separately as **Credit**.
- Unused credit is forfeited if your account is suspended or closed.
- Credit obtained against the rules can be reversed, and promotions can be changed or ended.

The [Terms](https://www.updown.fast/terms) set out the full promotion rules. See also [Legal and eligibility](/help/legal-and-eligibility).

## How you get it

Credit arrives on its own. Promotions need no claim.

When a promotion is running, the app shows the offer and its amount. Each account can get at most one of the three promotions (sign-up bonus, first-deposit match, covered trade), ever.

| Way | How it works | Notification |
|-----|-------------|-------------|
| **Sign-up bonus** | A new account gets credit when it's created. Once per account. | "Thanks for joining. It's used before your cash, and you keep the profit." |
| **First-deposit match** | Your **first** deposit is matched dollar for dollar in credit, up to the cap in the offer. An offer can set a minimum deposit, and a smaller deposit doesn't count as your first. Any deposit that reaches your balance counts: USDG on Robinhood Chain, a stablecoin moved over from another network, or our payment partner. Only deposits made while the promotion runs count. | "We matched your first deposit. It's used before your cash, and you keep the profit." |
| **Covered trade** | For accounts created while the promotion runs. Your first trade to finish decides: a window held to the close, or one you sold out of. If its cash result is a loss, you get credit equal to the cash you lost, up to the cap in the offer. A win gets nothing, and a break-even doesn't use the cover up. The cover still applies if the promotion ends later. | "Your first trade lost, so we covered it. It's used before your cash, and you keep the profit." |
| **Creator rewards** | Every 500 points you claim become $5.00 of trade credit. You claim them from the Rewards popup or the Creator reward card on Portfolio. See [Creator rewards](/platform/creator-rewards). | "You turned 500 pts into trade credit. It's used before your cash, and you keep the profit." |
| **From the updown team** | Occasional credits from the team. | "A credit from the updown team. It's used before your cash, and you keep the profit." |

Each notification is titled "$X trade credit added".

### Where offers show

- **Signed out:** a strip above the top bar on every page, for example "Your first trade is covered on us…", with a **Sign up** button. Its **×** hides it for that offer.
- **Signed in, on a phone:** a one-line banner above the trades on the **Home** tab. Its **×** hides it for that offer.
- A first-deposit offer has a **Deposit now** button that opens the deposit menu. See [Deposit and withdraw](/portfolio/deposit-withdraw).

## What it's spent on

Credit is used first, then cash, on:

- immediate interval buys from the ticket;
- each pick of a [Bundle](/interval/bundles).

It's never used on:

- limit orders;
- multiplier orders, which use cash only;
- orders placed through the [Trade API](/api/introduction).

When credit funds a buy, the "Trade executed" card reads, for example, "$3.00 credit + $2.00 cash · you keep the profit". The Bundle slip shows "Trade credit · used first", and its receipt reads "$X credit used · you keep the profit".

## What happens to it

Credit is pooled per window, across both sides.

| Event | What happens |
|-------|-------------|
| **Win at the close** | You keep your cash and get the profit: the payout less your whole stake, credit included. The credit is used up. Example: $5 of credit buys contracts that pay $10, so you get $5. |
| **Loss at the close** | The credit is lost. Only your own cash part of the stake is taken. |
| **Push** (the payout exactly equals the stake) | The credit comes back to your credit balance. |
| **Sold before the close at a profit** | Your cash stake comes back, plus the profit. The credit is used up. |
| **Sold at a loss or break-even** | What the sale brings refills your credit first, up to the credit you used, then your cash stake. |

For example, say you buy with $5 of cash and $5 of credit, then sell before the close:

| Sold for | What you get back |
|----------|-------------------|
| $4 | $4 back as credit, and no cash |
| $10 | $5 back as credit, and $5 cash |
| $12 | $7 cash. The credit is used up. |

Before you sell, **Live Positions** previews the split: "Sell ≈ $X back as credit · $Y to wallet", or "Sell ≈ $X credit used (profit) · $Y to wallet". After a sale that returns credit, the "Trade executed" card reads "$X back as trade credit".

<Note>
Your P&L counts the cash result. That applies to **Realized P&L**, the Portfolio chart, and the leaderboards. A loss funded by credit counts only the cash you lost. A win counts its profit.
</Note>

## Where your credit shows

- **Credit** in the top bar, on desktop, while you have some;
- the Portfolio trade-credit line: "$A trade credit · $B in positions · not withdrawable — the profit is". A is your spendable credit, and B is credit in open positions;
- "+ $Y credit" beside **Buying power** in **Live Positions**;
- "Trade credit · used first" on the Bundle slip.
