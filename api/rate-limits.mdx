## REST

Each client IP address gets a budget of **20 requests per second**, with bursts of up to **40**. Every response tells you where you stand:

| Header | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `UpDown-RateLimit-Remaining` | Requests left in your burst right now. |
| `UpDown-RateLimit-Reset` | Seconds until your burst is full again. |
| `Retry-After` | On a `429` only: seconds to wait before retrying. |

Over the limit, you get HTTP `429` with code `RATE_LIMITED`. Wait `Retry-After` seconds, then continue.

**New orders** have their own budget per API key: **10 per second**, bursts of **20**. A [batch](/api/orders#many-at-once-batch) counts one per item it places; an item past the budget is answered `RATE_LIMITED` in its own result while the rest of the batch stands. **Cancels are never limited** — alone or in a batch, you can always pull your orders.

### API tiers

Trade more and your keys get a higher order budget. Your tier is set once a day (UTC) from every contract your account traded in the last 30 days, maker or taker.

| Tier | Contracts in the last 30 days | New orders per key | Keyed reads per key |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | — | 10/s, bursts of 20 | 10/s, bursts of 20 |
| Advanced | 10,000 | 20/s, bursts of 40 | 20/s, bursts of 40 |
| Pro | 100,000 | 50/s, bursts of 100 | 100/s, bursts of 200 |
| Premier | 1,000,000 | 100/s, bursts of 200 | 200/s, bursts of 400 |

Each key has its own budget at your tier. Your tier and how far you are from the next one are on your [Portfolio](https://www.updown.fast/portfolio) page, under **API tier**. If we've set a custom limit for your account, it applies instead of the table, shared by all your keys.

Failed API-key attempts have their own, much smaller budget. An address that keeps sending invalid keys is locked out for at least a minute and gets `429` until then.

## Websocket

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Connections per IP address | 20 |
| Subscriptions per connection | 100 |
| Commands per connection | 10 per second, bursts of 20 |
| Largest message you can send | 64 KB |

## Staying under the limits

- **Use the websocket for anything live.** One connection with a series subscription replaces polling every market, and updates arrive as they happen.
- Use REST for a one-off read or to seed state, then follow changes on the websocket.
- Back off on `429`; don't retry in a tight loop.
