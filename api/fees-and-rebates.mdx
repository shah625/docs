updown charges a fee only on the **taker** side of a fill. If your order was **resting** on the book when another trader's order filled against it, you are the **maker**: you pay nothing, and you earn a **maker rebate**, a share of the fee the taker paid.

The rebate is automatic. There is nothing to sign up for or turn on. It applies to every resting order you place, through the API or on the website.

<Note>
The program's full terms are the [API and Liquidity Provider Agreement](https://www.updown.fast/liquidity-providers). By using the API or resting an order on the book, you agree to it.
</Note>

## Maker and taker

| | You are the… | Fee | Rebate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your order fills against an order already resting on the book | **Taker** | Taker fee | — |
| Another order fills against your resting order | **Maker** | `0` | A share of that taker's fee |

An order that crosses the book when you place it is a taker for the part that fills at once. Whatever is left rests, and is a maker from then on. Set [`post_only: true`](/api/orders#place-an-order) to make sure an order only ever rests: if any of it would trade at once, it is refused with `POST_ONLY_CROSS`.

## The taker fee

Each series publishes its fee in [`fee_schedule`](/api-reference/list-series), as a formula in `p`, the fill price in dollars:

```json
"fee_schedule": {
  "maker": "0",
  "taker": "min(0.07 * p * (1 - p), 0.0175) per contract"
}
```

The fee is largest at 50¢ (1.75¢ per contract) and falls toward zero near 1¢ and 99¢. It is charged in dollars and rounded to the millionth of a dollar. A taker order's fees show in its `fees_paid`. On your maker fills it stays `"0"`.

Read `fee_schedule` from the API rather than hard-coding it. It can change.

## Maker tiers

From **November 12, 2026 at 12:00 PM ET**, your share of the taker fee depends on your **maker tier**: how many contracts your resting orders filled against other traders in the 30 days before each day (UTC).

| Tier | Contracts made (30 days) | `crypto` | `stocks`, `preipo`, `commodities` |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | Any | 15% | 20% |
| Tier 1 | 1,000 | 30% | 40% |
| Tier 2 | 10,000 | 40% | 50% |
| Tier 3 | 50,000 | 50% | 60% |
| Tier 4 | 250,000 | 60% | 70% |

- **Only maker fills count.** Contracts you made against other traders, the same fills that earn a rebate. Your taker volume doesn't move your maker tier.
- **Your tier is set once a day.** It is worked out at the start of each day (UTC). A day's tier is the higher of that day's and the previous day's, so the minutes before the daily update keep yesterday's tier.
- **It only goes up during a day.** A rebate you've already earned is never reduced.
- **Onboarded market makers** never earn less than Tier 1.
- **See where you stand** on your [Portfolio](https://www.updown.fast/portfolio) and [API keys](https://www.updown.fast/api-keys) pages, under **Rebate tier**: your tier, and how far you are from the next one.

Markets in other categories earn no rebate for now. A change to the rebates applies only to fills after it takes effect.

**Example.** A taker buys 100 contracts from your resting order at 50¢ on a crypto market. The taker pays 100 × 0.07 × 0.50 × 0.50 = **$1.75** in fees. You pay nothing and earn your tier's share: at Tier 1, 30% of it, **$0.525**; at Tier 4, 60%, **$1.05**.

### Before maker tiers

Until November 12, 2026 at 12:00 PM ET, every fill earns the same share: 30% on `crypto` and 40% on `stocks`, `preipo` and `commodities`. Each fill earns the share in force when it filled.

## Which fills earn

A fill earns a rebate when the taker is another trader in good standing. It earns nothing when the other side of the fill is your own account, or an account linked to yours (a [team](/platform/team-access) member of your account, or an account you are a member of). [Self-trade prevention](/api/errors) stops your order from filling against your own resting order. Don't rely on it as your only control.

The agreement lists who can earn and the conduct rules. Wash trading, spoofing, or splitting activity across accounts to earn more voids rebates.

## Getting paid

Rebates are **paid automatically**. There is nothing to claim.

- About once a minute, everything you have earned and not yet been paid is sent to your updown wallet as cash.
- Payouts go out in whole cents, so an amount under 1¢ waits for the next payout.
- Payouts need the current Terms of Service accepted.
- Your **Portfolio** page shows your total rebates beside **Joined**. Open it for a log by day: what you earned, and each payout with its transaction.

There is no API endpoint for rebates yet. Your [API keys page](https://www.updown.fast/api-keys) also shows what you have earned each day.

## Program start

The program has run since **October 17, 2026** (midnight US Eastern). Every eligible maker fill from then on earns.
